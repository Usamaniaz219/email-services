If you're going to make us part of your daily tea routine, you should understand how we make our choices.

**Our curation methodology:**

Every tea in our collection goes through a structured evaluation process. We work directly with tea estates and verified importers, requesting samples and documentation before any tea qualifies for membership boxes.

**What we evaluate:**

- Cultivation practices and estate certifications
- Processing methods and quality control standards
- Flavor profile and how it represents its category
- Value relative to the retail market
- Seasonal availability and supply chain reliability

**Our team:**

Your curation is led by Lin Zhao, who spent eight years in tea procurement for specialty retailers before joining The Steep Club. She's supported by a tasting panel that includes certified tea specialists and members who provide feedback on each selection.

**Why this approach:**

We built The Steep Club because we found the tea market lacked structured information. Many subscriptions focus on novelty over quality assessment. We focus on providing consistent quality you can rely on every month and helping you understand what makes each tea worth your attention.

**Your membership includes:**

- Detailed tasting notes with specific brewing parameters
- Origin information with estate or producer names
- Quality indicators you can verify
- Guidance on storage and optimal consumption windows

If you value transparency and informed decision-making in your tea experience, The Steep Club provides that foundation.
