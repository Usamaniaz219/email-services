Whether you join The Steep Club or not, understanding tea quality helps you make better decisions for your everyday drinking.

**Three indicators of quality tea:**

**1. Leaf appearance**  
Quality loose-leaf tea shows consistent leaf size within a batch, minimal broken pieces, and clear visual characteristics of its type. Whole leaves or intentionally shaped leaves indicate careful processing.

**2. Aroma complexity**  
Before brewing, quality tea offers distinct aromatics. After brewing, the scent should develop additional layers. Single-note or flat aromas often indicate older tea or lower-grade material.

**3. Multiple infusions**  
Quality leaves withstand multiple steepings while maintaining flavor. If tea becomes bitter or flavorless after one infusion, it suggests lower grade or improper processing.

**Why brewing parameters matter:**

Each tea category requires specific water temperature and steeping duration. Using boiling water for green tea or under-steeping black tea masks the actual quality. We provide tested parameters for every tea we send because proper brewing reveals what you're actually tasting.
