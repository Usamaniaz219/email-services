# The Steep Club Launch Campaign

## 1. Campaign Overview

**Campaign Name**: "Your Steep Club Invitation"

**Summary**: Convert 300 waitlist members into paying subscribers through a 4-week email campaign featuring segment-specific messaging, early access incentives, and educational content that builds confidence in the subscription value.

**Primary Objective**: Convert 300 out of 2,000 waitlist members (15% conversion rate) to active subscriptions within the first month of launch.

**Secondary Objectives**:
- Achieve 50%+ email open rates by leveraging waitlist anticipation
- Drive 25% click-through rate on subscription offers
- Collect feedback from non-converters to inform future campaigns

---

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary Audience Segments

**Segment 1: Tea-Curious Beginners** (estimated 35% of waitlist)
- New to loose-leaf tea or tea subscriptions
- Pain points: Overwhelmed by tea variety, don't know where to start, worried about waste if they don't like it
- Motivations: Want to explore tea culture, looking for guidance and education
- Key message need: Simplicity, curation, no-risk trial

**Segment 2: Gift Buyers** (estimated 25% of waitlist)
- Looking for thoughtful, ongoing gifts for others
- Pain points: Generic gifts feel impersonal, hard to find gifts for people who have everything
- Motivations: Want to give something unique and recurring that shows they care
- Key message need: Gift-worthy presentation, flexibility, recipient delight

**Segment 3: Daily Tea Drinkers** (estimated 40% of waitlist)
- Already drink tea regularly, know their preferences
- Pain points: Running out of tea, limited local selection, paying premium prices at retail
- Motivations: Convenience, variety, discovering new high-quality teas
- Key message need: Quality, value, convenience, discovery

### Where They Are
- **Channel**: Email (they opted into the waitlist)
- **Buying stage**: Consideration → Decision (they've already expressed interest)
- **Decision factors**: Price, tea quality, curation vs. choice, subscription flexibility

## 3. Key Messages

### Core Campaign Message
"The Steep Club is now open—your curated tea experience starts here."

### Supporting Messages by Segment

**For Tea-Curious Beginners:**
1. **"We'll guide you."** — Every box includes tasting notes, brewing tips, and stories behind each tea
2. **"Start small, explore big."** — Begin with our starter plan and discover what you love without commitment
3. **"No tea knowledge required."** — Our curation experts pick teas that represent the best of each style
4. **Proof points**: "92% of new tea drinkers say they feel more confident brewing tea after their first box"

**For Gift Buyers:**
1. **"A gift that keeps giving."** — Monthly delivery of thoughtfully curated teas with beautiful presentation
2. **"They'll think of you every morning."** — Create a lasting connection with a gift they use daily
3. **"Flexible gifting, no hassle."** — Pause, skip, or cancel anytime; gift messaging included
4. **Proof points**: "Rated the #1 tea gift subscription by [publication]" or "Over 10,000 gift subscriptions sent"

**For Daily Tea Drinkers:**
1. **"Stop running out."** — Never face an empty tea tin again with monthly deliveries
2. **"Discover beyond your favorites."** — Our tea hunters source rare and exceptional teas you won't find locally
3. **"Better value, better tea."** — Premium loose-leaf tea delivered at prices below specialty retail
4. **Proof points**: "Average savings of $X per month vs. buying retail" or "Access to teas from 15+ countries"

### Call to Action Variations
- **Urgency**: "Join in the next 48 hours and get [early member perk]"
- **Exclusivity**: "As a waitlist member, you get first access"
- **Low friction**: "Start your subscription—pause or cancel anytime"

## 4. Channel Strategy

### Email (Primary Channel)

**Why this channel**: 
- Direct line to a warm, opted-in audience
- Enables segmentation for personalized messaging
- Low cost, high control
- Waitlist members expect to hear from you

**Content formats**:
- **Announcement emails**: Launch news, early access windows
- **Educational emails**: How to brew, tea origin stories, subscription value breakdown
- **Social proof emails**: Customer stories, reviews, behind-the-scenes
- **Reminder/urgency emails**: Limited-time offers, early bird perks ending

**Effort level**: Medium (requires copywriting, design, segmentation, automation setup)

**Estimated send volume**: 8-10 emails per subscriber over 4 weeks (2-3 per week)

### Owned Channels (Supporting)

**Website/Landing Page**
- Update homepage to announce launch
- Create dedicated subscription landing page with segment-specific value props
- Add FAQs addressing common objections
- Effort: Medium (one-time setup, minor updates)

**Social Media (Organic)**
- Announce launch to existing followers
- Share user-generated content from early subscribers
- Amplify email messages with visual content
- Effort: Low (3-5 posts per week)

## 9. Risks and Mitigations

### Risk 1: Waitlist fatigue or email list decay
**Likelihood**: Medium  
**Impact**: High (lower open/conversion rates)  
**Mitigation**: 
- Launch within 2-3 months of waitlist signup to maintain interest
- Use compelling subject lines and preview text
- Start with high-value content (not just promotional)
- Clean list: send re-engagement email to inactive addresses before campaign

### Risk 2: Segment assumptions are wrong
**Likelihood**: Medium  
**Impact**: Medium (wasted effort on wrong messaging)  
**Mitigation**:
- A/B test subject lines and CTAs in Week 1 to validate assumptions
- Monitor segment performance daily and adjust messaging in Week 2-3
- Include "not sure which plan is right for you?" option that asks questions
- Survey non-converters to understand why they didn't subscribe

### Risk 3: Early bird perk isn't compelling enough
**Likelihood**: Low-Medium  
**Impact**: High (slower conversion, miss goal)  
**Mitigation**:
- Test offer before launch (survey waitlist on what would motivate them)
- Have backup offers ready (e.g., free upgrade, bonus tea sample, extended trial)
- Monitor Week 1 performance; if conversion is <5%, introduce stronger offer in Week 2

### Risk 4: Technical issues (payment, website, email delivery)
**Likelihood**: Low  
**Impact**: High (lost conversions, poor experience)  
**Mitigation**:
- Test full signup flow before launch (multiple devices, browsers)
- Test email deliverability (check spam scores, preview in multiple clients)
- Monitor payment processor for errors
- Have support plan ready for customer questions

## 10. Next Steps

### Immediate Actions (Before Week 1)
1. **Finalize segment assignment** — Tag waitlist members by segment (beginners, gifters, daily drinkers) based on signup source or survey data
2. **Write email copy** — Draft all Week 1-2 emails (6 emails total) and load into email platform
3. **Update landing page** — Ensure subscription page is ready with pricing, FAQs, and segment-specific messaging
4. **Set up tracking** — Implement UTM codes for email links, conversion tracking, segment tagging
5. **Test payment flow** — Complete end-to-end test subscription (and refund) to catch issues
6. **Create early bird offer** — Decide on perk (discount, bonus, upgrade) and set expiration
7. **Schedule emails** — Load Week 1 emails into platform with send dates/times

### Week 1 Actions
1. **Send launch email** (Monday morning, 9 AM local time)
2. **Monitor performance** — Check open rates, click rates, signups hourly on Day 1
3. **A/B test** — If open rates are low, test new subject lines for Wed/Fri emails
4. **Respond to replies** — Many people will reply with questions; respond quickly to build trust
5. **Adjust messaging** — If one segment is converting much better, consider reallocating effort

### Stakeholder Approvals Needed
- **Email copy approval** — Get sign-off on tone, offers, claims (especially if making guarantees)
- **Legal review** — Ensure subscription terms, cancellation policy, and auto-renewal disclosures are clear
- **Brand review** — Confirm messaging aligns with brand voice and values
- **Finance approval** — Confirm early bird discount or promo codes are properly configured

### Key Decision Points
1. **End of Week 1**: Are we on track for 100+ subs? If not, what needs to change?
2. **End of Week 2**: Which segment is performing best? Should we reallocate focus?
3. **Mid-Week 3**: Are we at 200+ subs? If not, consider extending early bird offer or introducing new incentive
4. **End of Week 4**: Did we hit 300? If yes, plan expansion. If no, what did we learn for next campaign?
