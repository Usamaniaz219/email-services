# Email Marketing Studio

This is a small business email marketing studio for planning and writing email campaigns.

## Folders

- **brand**: Brand voice guidelines and messaging rules
- **leads**: Customer lists and contact information
- **campaigns**: Email campaign plans and content
- **notes**: Working notes and ideas

## Studio Rules

1. Write everything in plain English
2. Never send email to real people (exception: sending through Resend is allowed only to Resend's test addresses ending in @resend.dev and to my own email address — a guard will enforce this)
3. Ask before deleting anything
